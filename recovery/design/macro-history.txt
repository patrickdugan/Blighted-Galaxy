Episode VIII

The Last Human

Amicus, the burgeoning Group-Mind/Protector of Transhumanity is fighting a losing war in the
Orion Arm against the radial expansion of The Blight. Engaging in systematic triage, it prepares
to abandon entire sectors to the radial expansion. However, a scout reports the location of the
last remaining population of Baseline Humans on a partially terraformed world. A mission is
undertaken: protect the vestigial population for the posterity of time, at all costs.

Episode X

A New Ambition

Transhumanity in the Orion's Arm face a fateful decision: to stay and die from an unending
bombardment, or to try and flee the galaxy. To adapt, Transhumanity begins evolving into
minitaturized nano-bodies as a way of preserving the mind and the sanity of an emigree. A fleet
of emissaries prepares to make their way off the galactic plane, towards distant stars in the
Canis Major Overdensity, taking advantage of the lesser harzards of the sparse, intergalactic
medium to achieve greater maximums speeds.

When they arrive, in 27,000 years, much history will have transpired.

When the light from those events reaches them, over 50,000 years will have passed.

Episode XII

The Fall of Olympus

The post-human Faction known as the Titans have taken to constructing a massive, cluster-
wide mega-engineering project, as they watch the stars go dim around them.

The Titan home system, Olympus, is home to a vast array of computronium used to process
sensor data from a 10 cubic parsec area. Their defenses include the best nanotechnology
devised within limits of entropy. However in their hubris, they have not considered simple facts
of geometry, such as their position in the local galaxy: completely surrounded.

Episode XVII

You Never Go Full Blight

The local quadrant of the Galaxy is half-way covered with Blight. Dim red light emerges from 85
degrees of visible sky.

The remants of Transhumanity have fled beyond The Outer Arm towards the galactic rim,
making homes where they may, hoping to evade destruction.

One settlement has existed for almost 500 years, hiding on a rocky planet, with minimal mega-
structure usage to give away their position. They use the light of the star sporadically, to
manufacture Kugelblitz Black Holes inside a subterreanean particle accelerator hidden under
the planet. With this achievement, Transhumanity's Remnant has reclaimed a powerful industria
capacity, the ability to make Halo Drives.

Space is so vast that long periods of peace can punctuate the brief moments where relativistic
Weapons Of Mass Destruction end billions of lives in the blink of an eye.

Episode XVIII

Patricia Tries

Millenia have passed since the great Parting of Ways.

Transhumanity's Remnant has wandered far, putting great distance between themselves and
the slow, but constant, expansion of The Blight. Around an unassuming red dwarf, they make
their new home, Bel-Air, and begin a procession of many millenia of peace to come. They are
protected by the large quantities of space and time that separate them from their would-be
exterminators.

Watching from afar, The Abominations, militant defectors from the Remnant, have been
observed building a large Dyson Swarm around a massive 0-type Blue Supergiant to the
galactic north, over 5000 light years away from Bel-Air. They have already begun producing and
deploying Black Hole Bombs used to project the Abominations at relativistic speeds towards
their targets, before the eventual High-Energy Dissolution of the Black Hole from Hawking
Radiation destroys the target star.

The grandchildren of the leader of the Remant, Captain Wicker, are born in Bel-Air, a pair of
twins. They are completely niave to the brutality that has preceeded them, but the light from the
distant Black Hole Bomb detonations will reach their eyes in time.

Blowing Up The Galaxy

Super-empowered by nanites supplied by the Patricia Tries civilization, Tatyana and Alfonso
make their way towards the galactic north, to intercept their Great Aunt.

The next thousand years enables the local nodes of the Blighted Swarm (a.k.a. "The Patricia
Tries Civilization") to adapt to the history of the First Contact and Battle of The Cluster Bombs,
which Alfonso barely diffused, transforming The Blight in unpredictable ways.

Now as the twins are greeted by the Swarm of a nearby star, an intelligence relay is delivered.
Great Aunt Weena is reported to be catalyzing a type IIb supernova from a nearby captured
supergiant, the resulting spinning black hole of 12 solar masses will enable the creation of the
ultimate weapon: The Chamber of Guf.

The twins begin to devise a plan, to military approach their Aunt's stronghold in the Nebular
Wastes, and prevent the completion of this weapon. If they fail, a high frequency series of
concentrated energy blasts, calculated with post-human intelligence, will pierce the heart of
every star in a cone of 175 degrees and 12,000 parsecs.

Episode XX

Death Star Hop-Scotch

The way the Patricia Tries civilization reacts to the Battle of Guf reverberates in unpredictably
diverging, branching patterns based on the geometry of consensus formation. Due to the slower
path of the message, to Blight Nodes on the far side of the Nebular Wastes, many Swarms are
preparing to launch counter-attacks, by concentrating the light of their stars into focused,
converging, co-ordinated attack patterns, filling the sky with a criss-cross of fiery death.

The destruction caused by the Chamber of Guf, continues to wreath through the galaxy. With no
time for adaptive response, or forewarning, all misses prove useless to the Swarms, and those
Swarms hit are severely damaged or destroyed. Never before has this scale of destruction been
visited to The Blight. The reaction will be extremely long-term, and the choice to stand-down or
counter-attack will be made differently, based on what the Patricia Trie state is in that system, in
a cascading domino reaction, for tens of thousands of years.

However, the new Alliance of the "Abominations", known refered to honorifically as "The
Formoi", and the Bel-Air Space force uses the one advantage they have - anticipation of the
attacks. A massive program of co-ordinated, randomized stellar movements is undertaken.

Episode XXI

Double-barreled White Dwarf Shotguns

Waves and waves of advanced mutative entities begins to sweep the Outer-Arm.
Transhumanity and various Post-human populations are growing in the protected sectors
beyond the Nebular Wastes. But the new Weapons of Mass Destruction are brought to bear. A
massive program of White Dwarf collection using Halo-drive ships to corral the obects
gravitationally, has been deployed by The Blight in response to the earlier war. Now, gigantic
avatars of the Swarms emerge weilding proportionately massive railguns, capable of launching
a pair of electro-degenerate objects in a swirling blast, towards a target, where they fuse
together and trigger a type Ia supernova.

There are 10 billion White Dwarf stars in the galaxy. One for every 800 cubic light years. A cone
300 light years wide, 200 light years thick and 1000 light years contains - on average - 75,000
White Dwarves. That's 37,500 Shotgun Shells.

Episode XXII

Amicus Brief

A new era of memetic warfare begins as the supernovas die-down, and the violence slows, the
Patricia Trie has updated. Now there are two distinct branches, one that has pervaded more to
the galactic north and south, another radiating from the old Orion Arm. One branch is shaped
only by the contact made over ten thousand years ago by Alfonso and Tatyana, the other is
shaped by the ancient encounter between a post-human group mind and early nodes of The
Blight.

Now it is as if Civil War has erupted between the two Prime Branches of the Patricia Tries. The
war is waged at the speed of light, via attempts to influence the consensus algorithm
underpinning the Swarms.

The first branch, wishes to end the war, attain a symbiotic balance with its neighbors, and serve
as a data and energy-service, based on the dreams of Alfonso. The second branch wishes to
assimilate again, all life, based on the dream of an ancient post-human.
