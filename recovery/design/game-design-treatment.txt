       Composite Gameplay

   1) Relativistic flight/flight duels/jousting
   2) Interstellar warfare with weapons of mass destruction/War Economy
   3) Transhuman/Post-human Duels

        The three modes of gameplay hinge on a universe where the speed of light is the limit of
movement, vast distances of space and time separate a series of points on a map. In Blighted
Galaxy 1, the map is 2d, in Blighted Galaxy 2, ideally, the map would be 3d reflecting the
physical grandeur of space. When it comes to plotting movements between stars, the geometry
of 3d space permits more radial degrees of approach, which in a game set in a giant elliptical
galaxy, would become a consistent risk. In a spiral galaxy, the depth of the galactic disk and the
higher density star population limits how much radial approach complicates long-term strategic
positioning. So one would hope that not so much of the geometry of the strategy game is
warped by transitioning from 2d to 3d.

   1) Relativistic duels, involve shooting beams at the speed of light while traveling at a lesser
      fraction. Changing trajectory while going to high sub-relativistic speeds is hard, so this
      sort of high speed flight, while thrilling, also has tank-like inertia to it, which is further
      warped by gravity, in fun, circular ways. Some UFO lore focuses on a craft’s ability to
      pivot on a dime at impressive speeds, and that might be physically plausible at the low
      velocities of a visible UFO, we’re talking about basis points of light speed and it busts a
      zig-zag, sure. Go for it. Use some tiny, unseen, high energy reaction to emit a burst of
      (presumably detectable energy), or use some yet-unknown magic-tech to reactionless
      zip around, the energy going into the nth dimension or something. It’s still energy,
      affecting inertia, to perform those pivots, this is basic Newtonian stuff. So it follows that
      maneuverability at 0.7c is limited. The ability to aim and fire, on the other hand, allows
      for a lot of geometry. There is a time-lag, because targets are at distances of several
      light seconds or minutes away. The ability to hit such a fine target, and aim a weapon at
      such mass (so accelerated, the pivoting could feel like spooning a neutron star), but let’s
      say transhuman/post-human abilities enable the aiming and firing, like an oblong
      kamehameha. We’re willing to grease the wheels on that detail. You end up with
      gameplay a bit like Joust or Tron, but with shooting, and the gravity of massive objects
      becomes a tactical terrain utilization option. This is kinda fun, but if we slow down...

   2) Transhuman Duels

        Ultimately, in the future of >100,000 years After Fresh, the various forms of the
descendants of humanity have had to converge on bio-nanoborg body types, which support
survival in extreme environments for long periods of time, scale adjustment, dynamic
construction of habitats, food ingredients, megastructures, body clones, forks if need be, things
that are needed in a Blighted Galaxy. So when two of these entities get down, it comes off
somewhere between the violent killing between chimpanzees at the start of 2001: A Space

Odyssey, the eldritch bludgeoning of an Evangelion battle, perhaps including the cannibalism,
and the geometry/chemistry/thermodynamics of nanomachines battling in space.

        In the Blighted Galaxy, there is no Superintelligence. There is only The Blight, a
graveyard of Failed Transcendence Experiments, and the clever physics-based explosives that
are possible when you build megastructures around supergiant stars. However there is a vast
range of cladistics from the various survivors of transhumanity, who have adapted in many
ways, becoming Space Dragons, the long-extinct Titans, the mysterious black hole engineering
project of the vanished Great Library Civilization, the reckless, loose transformation of the
Abominations that culminated in a spinning black hole becoming a jeweled mirror super-gun and
through-putting destructive beams to target millions of stars within a short time span.

         In this setting, to be a post-Singularity being is to have already incorporated a
gravitational singularity into your body-mind-megastructure architecture. Post-human
consciousness *is* different from transhuman consciousness, which is like a funky, relatable
version of we like to think of as human. Post-humans are utterly distinct, separated from other
cultures by thousands of light years and as much history. Yet, can one become post-human,
return to being more transhuman, even recast themselves as a synthetic baseline human?
Sure, if they wanted to. It’s just not something people have a ton of time for in this setting. What
with all the warfare.

        The tiering of the entities in the setting, in the sense of power level, has a lot of to do
with their size and the energy throughput of their megastructures. This is not a setting where the
protagonists are bright “modosophonts” against the arcane backdrop of a civilized galaxy with a
wormhole network enjoying the discourse of Singularity-level 1-6 minds. This is a setting, where
a transhuman protagonist stands down the blighting swarms over ten million stars in a radial,
dampening, incomplete sweep around the galactic arms. It is a setting where the horrifying,
unstoppable passage of time, and the horrifying, seemingly unstoppable expansion of
wavefronts, dominates intelligence, growth of thought, or any other such luxuries. Where
stupidity is slightly more probable to emerge out of the competitive percolation of a slow
communication galaxy. It’s about heavy metal synth fugues escalating the way physics does.

       A general taxonomy of “Power Levels” based on physics, not awareness, follows:

        0 - Biological lifeforms, totally vulnerable, gamma ray events increasing has rendering
large swaths of the galaxy sterile for future evolution of biological life, even millions of years
after these wars end.

         1 - Nearbaseline Transhumans, largely extinct, tried building relativistic fleets with lasers
to take on The Blight. Kind of got us all into this mess in the first place. Often have biological
core frames but symbiotic nanites, cybernetic interlace, supported by technology of a K-I
civilization (but not K-II, due to the long observed mutual agreement and mutually-assured
destruction that allowed the completely decentralized colonies to be at peace for 30 millenia.
Their run is ended.

       2 - Outer Arm Bionanoborgs

        The surviving descendents of Transhumanity, shoot them in the head, no big deal, blow
them up with a frag grenade, pop those shards right out. Blast them with a 20MW plasma
cannon, ok sure, dead. Can be backed up. Can transform. Can survive in space. Can pretend to
be as human as people were long ago, but it’s more of a cultural custom. Sort of an editor’s
curation of different post-human tech relics that managed to make their way outward ahead of
the Blight’s Expansion, because parents love their children sometimes, and want them to
survive.

       3 - Baseline Post-Human

        There is no homogenous baseline of what it means to be a post-human in terms of form
and architecture, but as a category, there is a general baseline of ability for someone who has
embraced the path of transformation and obtained some form of post-humanity. Some have
said, to be post-human, is to stop pretending. Abilities generally include: high-data-throughout
specialized cognition (process lots of sensors with some lag), transform body into a spaceship,
integrated fusion power, plasma control. Downsides of post-humanity run a tremendous gamut
but include: schizophrenic ego-fragmentation, tendencies to cloister for long-periods in
simulated environments, grandiose singular obsessions, omnicidal mania, suicidal tendencies
masked as “transcendence experimentation” that for all outside observers appears to be fancy
suicide, austere asceticism, tendencies to abuse less powerful life-forms, complete erosion of
capacity for empathy, and much more!

       The Abomination Bombers are Baseline Post-Humans who use Hawking-class black
holes as propellant and then as a final kamikaze detonation when they arrive at target. Flying
such a mission as a fork of a perpetually self-replicating warrior is typical baseline post-human
stuff.

       4 - Stellar Swarm

        At this scale, relativistic latency between different points in a solar-scale swarm of
rotating habitats or computing objects becomes the limiting factor in communication and mind-
growth. Group-minds delegating different spatial regions of a swarm have been attempted, but
what seems to dominate life in the galaxy is this basic data structure with an extreme
redundancy, a cryptographically verifiable lineage, trickling around in waves of updates, allowing
for basic decisions to be made comprehensively as a singular decision, but over tens of
thousands of years of relays. Most of the Stellar Swarms in the galaxy use star-scale energy,
but for arguably meaningless purposes, like zombies. Yet, these sort of zombies are particularly
hostile when agitated. The energy of the star can be used to propel the system into new
trajectories, or concentrated into an offensive blast, or used in lighter concentrations to
accelerate allies and provide artillery support over the surrounding area, where accuracy is

        The story of the zombified Swarms is the story of Blighted Galaxy, but the stories of the

      5 - Trans-Singularity Post-humans

        Taking a Hawking-class Black Hole of a more long-lived mass into their form as a core-
engine, this tier of post-human has access to tremendous energy, can sustain the black hole
through periodic feedings, is capable of rapid deceleration or acceleration, can blast anything,
and can use the energy for computation to experience the galaxy as a vivid, integrated
psychedelic experience layered with all kinds of background thought. This heightened
consciousness enables them to execute physical maneuvers and sustained attacks involving
precise manipulation of the heaviest astrophysical objects in the universe. It’s like, they really
feel every swing of the neutron star. They really sense the precision of targeting a million
targets. Combat to them is an orgiastic celebration of physical reality that punctuate the quiet
simulation-binging-and-chill of the lonely night.

          The Abomination Bombers don’t quite qualify as this level because their Hawking-class
black holes are smaller, set to explode within 20 or 100 years, while the sustainable utilization of
black hole engines by this tier of entity, with typical active lifetimes of 500 or 1000 years, they
aren’t stuck on an accelerating trajectory until death, they can fully use the energy throughput of
the Hawking radiation for maximal energy usage in all aspects of life: thought, combat, and
flight. It also provides them a last-option maneuver if they are in a doomed situation.

        The Blight eventually learns from multiple wars with post-humans how to construct
similarly engineered superscale warriors capable of, among other things, running crew on
megascale Double Barreled White Dwarf Shotguns. This escalation in cleverness and force is
what happens when you fight the Blight and don’t happen to successfully scourge it from the
entire galaxy. It always bounces back harder.

      6 - Fused or Ascended Transingularity Post-Humans

         The Titans of the Perseus arm were rumored to have blasted the song of their struggle
out towards the entire rimward population, as a sort of forewarning some might hear. They
leveraged all their powers as Trans-singularity peeps, were defeated by military principles, they
lacked first strike advantage and ended up being boxed in. In the final battle, after triggering
multiple type Ia supernovae, fighting off advancing swarms from every direction, they resorted to
Fusions. By adding spin energy to their cores during the Fusion, and doubling their nanite mass,
the Titans hoped to achieve an exponential increase in their power output. In the process, the
two minds would have to fuse psychically, delegating control of different parts of the large new
body, which required a painful, but necessary, melding of egos and emergence of new forms of
intuitive control between the Two. Ultimately the Ultimate Warrior, resulting from the successive

fusing of all the Titans into the Colossus, enabled a spray of relativistic jets to repel the
oncoming swarms. However in the process of mentally fusing, for an unknown reason, the
Colossus imploded on itself, leaving just the spinning black hole in its wake, the last remnant of
the Titan K-2.2 civilization.

        The Great Library Civilization, a K-2.1 civilization (occupying a single cluster), is fabled
to have engineered a multi-black hole N-body system that, to this megasecond, continues to
feed on the chaotically infalling neighboring stars, the surrounding energy then provides a
region of space-time that is so contorted, no swarm or explorer dares approach it, and
gravitational lensing give observers a spastic lavalamp to try and analyze. Maybe they did it.
Maybe there’s really something amazing computationally going on in there.

         Part of the reason that Transhumanity has failed to resist The Blight, is stupidity, another
part is slow response, or lack of forewarning. But a big part of the reason why Post-Humanity of
various stripes hasn’t succeeded in winning, is because the relative simplicity of The Blight
makes it less failure prone. Every civilization who has tried to break through to something
Beyond Trans-Singularity status, has vanished from observable spacetime. People assume,
they all just fell into the black holes. But not everyone.

          In the same way that the Abomination Bombers use a high-energy, suicide-mission
version of Tier 5 Trans-singularity tech, but are themselves merely Tier 3 Baseline Post-humans
flying a mission of destiny, but they don’t get to experience a Hawking Radiation-powered brain
or any of the related tech of those older civs, Aunt Weena uses a spinning black hole as a
weapon, but doesn’t try to use it to create a great-entity or build a Cauchy-horizon based
computing substrate. She probably would have been fine running the spinner for a gigasecond,
setting up the better part of the stars in the galaxy for a probably intersection, and then suiciding
by falling into the black hole, because why not. So what’s the difference between being a
nihilistic terrorist trying to just blow things up, and suiciding out of nihilism, vs. losing causal
contact with the outside galaxy trying to build a black-hole computer. One is clearly nerdier than
the other.

         So getting back to duels. If you duel something of a higher tier, odds are you’re going to
be outmatched. That’s 101. Alfonso in Paticia Tries rages on a bomber, does the relativistic duel
to knock it around, as things slow the form of conflict shifts. He transforms and out-abominable-
izes the Abomination, but in a class Vishnu style. Empowered by the nanites from the Swarm
(tier 4), Alfonso and Tatyana become effectively a Transhuman “Superior”-clade version of a tier
3 being, but also a weird Blight Swarm-influenced hybrid because they carry and analyze a copy
of the Patricia Trie now. They grow into their powers over the subsequent arc of the stories.
Unlike a zombie apocalypse logistics, where you need to be quiet, manage the flow of
coagulating large swarms, and there’s no real cure or reasoning with the enemy, the Blight
Apocalypse has this weird dimension of memes transforming the Blight into a more layered and
culturally imprinted, pan-galactic zombie nanoswarm.

       You can be a fully-fledged space weirdo with sick powers, who spends a long time flying
through space, and not lose your mind. You don’t have to adopt crazy monster aesthetics. This
is where cosmetic preference and different clade strengths and weaknesses come in.

        At this level, the game design tradition of groups of heroes vs. boss monsters, or
individual RPG characters vs. a long-held nemesis of comparable power, is a necessary import.
Hit points are a mainstay. Want to get away from hit points, have some kind of anatomy-based
damage with various organ-failure dying pathologies. Doesn’t make a ton of sense for a
nanoborg battle. HP with a high regen-rate makes sense. It takes a certain amount of energy
throughput for tissues of nano-fabricated material to repair and re-organize. The regeneration
rate could be higher for a higher energy entity.

        As two entities decelerate and get closer, gravity between them becomes more relevant,
sharp energetic pulses can allow for pivots, and proximity is a count-down toward an
increasingly escalated violence that will bring the duel to a quick end. A more powerful entity
may become more vulnerable if lesser assailants get close, because sabotage of its structure
becomes possible. The only way physically to overcome the most-powerful trans-singularity
entities, is to bring a heavy mass object with you that can disrupt the movement of the black
hole powering the entity.

        When it comes to close range resolutions, a violent one will tend to fall to the higher-
energy entity, unless the lesser entities have significant numbers of succeed in sabotage. At
longer ranges, the ability to wield weapons of mass destruction become a probabilistic trump
card.

        The feeling of welding superweapons, triggering supernovas and stuff, is a key part of
the setting, more on that below, but also the feeling of flight, of going so fast that time dilation is
notable, is a key part of the experience. The composite gameplay of having high speed but
limited maneuverability, vs. slowing down and duking it out, is key to this feeling of extreme
inertia and de/acceleration.

         RPG elements, the cursed phrase, may enter in here to lend tactical complexity to the
combat. Aleatory elements, variable damage, can figure in. There’s a sense that raw brute-force
computational power (not to be confused with superintelligence) can program nanomachines to
route victoriously through the patterns of lattice arrayed by the opponent’s nanomachines. So
you get a sort of HP-regen rate/attack power factor in the energy throughput of a being, and an
Intelligent/MP attack power factor in its nano-crochet computing skillz.

         The non-aleatory damage-per-second dimension would be the Wattage rating of the
entity in question. Here we degrade into pure Dragon Ball Z kamehameha wave tugs-of-war or a
more radial light show alternative.

      Creative transformations into different structures make these tactical trade-offs into
something of an already-caught Pokemon menagerie, so intransitive relationships between

        Meme combat is the real MP lateral dimension, and raw computational power is
orthagonal to the mind-complexity that allows an entity to resist and dish-out seductive memes
that can have a number of effects. An Evangelion angel-possession type contact could make a

maybe does this, as seemed to be happening when Weena was attacked, leading her to
abandon transhumanism. A softer style of memetic attack can attempt to de-escalate conflict
through diplomatic persuasion.

       Wouldn’t it be cool if the mental pathologies of post-humans became a weak spot?

        Like in Vampire: The Masquerade, different clades can have different powers but also
different psychological pathologies to try and exploit.

        Dramatic arcs involving strange, multi-ten-thousand year processes of personal
evolution and the gyres and pitfalls of that process, turned into a symbolic game system for
trying to push entities’ buttons.

    3) Interstellar war economy

       First off, space-time is big. Space is big because it takes a lot of time to get anywhere.
You can spend centuries zipping around a theater of war in a cube 50 or 60 light years on each
side. You can spend millenia moving from one sector of the galaxy to another. The vast
distances separating us in spacetime, the improbability of contact, the beauty and peace, and
funky gloom of the Blight-light, all add to the brutal geometry of interstellar war. All stars are
weapons of mass destruction. Their mirror-focused attacks move at the speed of light, they can
be easily dodged by luck, but only evaded with a fore-known program of expecting these
attacks. First Strike Doctrine dominates interstellar war. That’s part of how the Blight got its
edge, though it expands at only ~60% of light speed. It’s edge had to be renewed by the lapses
in preparatory judgement and the compounded risk of desperate transcendence experiments.

        There’s a few key economic unit operations at these large time-scales:

    1) Capture a stellar remnant
    2) Nano-eat the matter of a solar system and build a swarm around the star

      war swarm
   4) Build a mega-structure capable of somehow utilizing a stellar remnant
   5) Build a mega-structure capable of housing transhumans
   6) Combine system nodes in a co-ordinated fashion
   7) Use a megastructure and star to manufacture Hawking-class black holes with
      Kugelblitzes.
   8) Use a swarm/megastructure to crunch research points to climb the tech tree

         The tech tree doesn’t need to be so innovative, the range of tech is not so great, this is a
setting where the physical limits of astrophysics energy gets thrown down in heavy metal
survival contests, so the tech tree mostly has to do with covering that territory. The Singularity-
like acceleration flow of Alpha Centauri blended with the modular composite unit-access of They
Are Billions.

       Physics Prototype:

       There are a few key things to get right about the micro-gameplay in a prototype:

   1) Feeling of flying and shooting at sub-relativistic speeds with light-time lags, zooming in,
      having gravitational effects as one decelerates.
   2) Ideally the physics of a black hole bomb going off and if it’s not close enough, the
      nanoswarm and/or the star’s mass (big range difference in that) gets tidally sheared.
   3) The close-up Transhuman dueling. SC 2 used a lot of goofy geometry to change up ship
      shooting patterns, also a closed 2-space so the velocity actually created a “phantom
      gravity” to speed-steer through the bullet hell. A flanderized electromagnetic locking
      device could be used to make this effect tangible, otherwise if we wanted to Gran
      Turismo these astrophysics, gravity between two small bodies really isn’t so big unless
      at Face Off proximities. Normal gravity does get us a fateful slug-out to the death within
      the last few km of proximity. But when it’s millions and billions of kilometers, we need to
      speed this thing along, otherwise you’ll be looping your opponent for millions of years
      before the end of the duel.
