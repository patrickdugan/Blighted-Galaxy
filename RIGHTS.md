# Rights and source provenance

This public repository is an archival and software-reconstruction project.

- The original fiction, macro-history, and game-design treatment were supplied by their author as historical creative drafts. Copyright and other rights remain with their original rights holders. They are not offered under the code's license.
- The newly written Rust implementation is a fresh reconstruction, based on the design documents and the author's recollection. Its redistribution license remains undecided. Cargo publishing is disabled.
- Previously lost engine code, original binary assets, and DOCX illustrations have not been recovered into this repository. Avoid attributing the clean-room code to the earlier implementation.
- The historical texts are transcriptions; do not silently rewrite them for continuity or physical accuracy. Preserve source files unchanged and record edited canon in a separate directory.
