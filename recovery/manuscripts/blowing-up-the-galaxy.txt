       Alfonso and Tatyana have spent entirely too much time together.

        Over nine thousand years have passed since the two left behind their childhood home,
Bel-Air, to investigate the war between their Great Aunt, Weena, her brigade of post-human
black hole bombers, and the sweeping Blight that covers much of the near-galaxy.

        There are only so many games, Quincy Jones songs, and re-plays of Fresh Prince
episodes, only so many conversations about chilling philosophical conundrums. They have
recorded songs together, split up over creative differences, and returned to solo recording, over
and over, like a Poincare recurrence of musical Supergroup formation and ejection. The
anesthetic feeling of operating on a tiny chip, accelerated to >70% of light-speed, one a
1/1000th time-scale simulation, was the debauched distraction of the majority of their real-time
lives, but only ~10% of their subjective lives. It took them 6578 years to travel the 5000 light
years from Bel-Air to beyond the war-front. But at 1/1000th time, most of that was experienced
as about six and a half years. It’s like they went to college and grad school together.

        Now they are changed. The ultimate form of interstellar travel is: first “become” “pure”
information, encode yourself as a computer program, etch it into a beam of light, and travel at
light speed, weightless, amazed that there are no mind-body relationships fundamental to
cognition that make this a pipe dream. Or etch into a chip, with a pack of nanites in your back
pocket, and build a giant computer that could receive a copy of your old civilization on a beam of
light.

        This process of copying, signalling, and reinforcing updates, is how the Patricia Tries
Civilization works.

      But ignoring mind-body gestalt consciousness is the blind way of The Blight, the Twins
had compromised and shrunk their bio-nano-borg bodies down to a more accelerated mass,
compressing their yet-human minds into tiny crystals of computronium.
